Good to know

Booking, plainly.

  • Booking ahead

    A day or two is usually enough. There are only eight mats, so the same day can be full. Book a week ahead in high season and for the full moon.

  • Paying

    Cash, bank transfer or Thai QR, before the session or when you arrive.

  • The Regulars

    ฿2,000 for four sessions, valid one year and not tied to your name. Details in The Regulars.

  • Private and villa sessions

    Any date, at the studio from ฿2,500 for two, or at your villa or hotel from ฿4,000 for two. See private and villa sound healing.

  • Handpan lessons

    Arranged around your stay rather than fixed to a day. See handpan lessons.
