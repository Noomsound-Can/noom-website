Who we are

Two people,
one terrace.

Noom Sound Studio is Can and Melie. Melie also runs House of Holistic. We hold the sessions together on the Noom Studio terrace in Lamai.

Can teaches handpan, rhythm and music theory, and has taught more than fifty students, taking three or four new ones each month. Melie has taught sound therapy to more than fifty students, and continues her practice as a yoga teacher, Tok Sen master and sound practitioner. We play regularly at venues such as Kamalaya, Anantara Lawana, 5 Elements, W and Conrad, and we hold a place in the Kamalaya wellness programme.

We play and teach from how sound actually works. Which notes sit well together, why a bowl keeps ringing after you let it go, how a room changes what you hear. Nothing here has to be a mystery to move you.

Intuition needs something to work with. You learn the notes, the intervals, the way a rhythm holds, and only then can you stop counting and simply play. What sounds like instinct is knowledge that has settled.

You need no experience. Come and play with us, or come and listen and do nothing at all. Either way, come as you are.

01

About
sound journeys.

What actually happens in a session?
You lie down on a mat, close your eyes, and rest while we play live instruments: handpan, gong, and Tibetan and crystal bowls. That is it. No participation required. Most people fall somewhere between awake and asleep for the whole thing.
How many people are in a session?
Eight mats, so eight people. It stays small on purpose.
What happens if it rains?
The terrace is covered, so sessions run. Rain on the roof is part of it.
Do I need any experience or background?
None at all. You do not need to know anything about music or sound work. If you can lie still for an hour, you are ready.
What does it feel like?
Different for everyone. Some people feel deeply relaxed. Some notice their mind going quiet. Some feel nothing unusual and just leave feeling rested. All of those are fine outcomes.
Is it like meditation?
Not exactly. You do not have to focus or follow any instructions. The sound does most of the work. You just show up and let it.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes, and plenty of people do.
What should I wear and bring?
Loose, comfortable clothes. We provide mats. Bring a light layer if you run cold, because lying still for an hour can make you cooler than expected.
Should I eat beforehand?
Eat light. A full meal right before makes it harder to relax.
Are there any reasons I shouldn’t come?
If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a recent surgery, a hearing condition, or significant sound sensitivity, message us first and we will figure out what works for you.

02

About
handpan lessons.

Do I need musical experience to learn handpan?
No. Most people who come to us have never played an instrument before. The handpan is built so that almost everything you play sounds good. There are no wrong notes in the traditional sense.
How quickly can I learn?
Most people leave their first session able to play simple patterns that actually sound like music. It is one of the fastest instruments to get started on.
Private or group, which is better for me?
Private lessons are 60 minutes, one student, full focus, good if you want to move at your own pace or have specific goals. Group lessons are 90 minutes for two or three people, better if you are coming with a friend or travel companion.
Do I need my own instrument?
No. We have instruments you can play during the session. If you are thinking about buying one, we are happy to talk you through that too.
Where do lessons take place?
At the Noom Studio open-air terrace in Lamai, or we can come to your hotel, villa, or chosen spot across Samui.
I am only in Samui for a few days. Is one lesson worth it?
Yes. A single session is enough to learn the basics and leave with something you can keep practicing, even without an instrument.
