Who we are

Who we are — Can and Melie,
sound therapists in Lamai

Noom Sound Studio is Can and Melie. Melie also runs House of Holistic. We hold the sessions together on the Noom Studio terrace in Lamai.

Can started playing handpan in 2016 and began teaching in 2024. He is certified in Integral Sound Healing by the Academy of Sound Healing (2024) and in Advanced Sound Facilitation by Shantika Academy (2025). He has held over one hundred sessions, in Turkey and here. He is Turkish, and moved to Samui in June 2025 to spend his time on handpan and sound therapy instead of a commercial career.

Melie studied public health and worked in hospitality before she trained as a yoga teacher in Koh Samui, where she holds a 200-hour Vikasa Yoga certification. She first heard sound healing during that training. She then completed a 100-hour Shantika sound therapy course, and has taught yoga at Shantika Academy to more than fifty students. She plays gong, crystal bowls and Tibetan bowls, and runs House of Holistic by Melie: sound therapy, yoga, guided meditation and Tok Sen massage.

We have played together for over a year, more than fifty sessions so far. We have been invited to play at Kamalaya, Anantara Lawana, W, Conrad, Absolute Sanctuary, Samujana, Vikasa, 5 Elements and Samaya Wellness, and we hold a place in the Kamalaya wellness programme. More about us.

We play and teach from how sound actually works. Which notes sit well together, why a bowl keeps ringing after you let it go, how a room changes what you hear. Nothing here has to be a mystery to move you.

Intuition needs something to work with. You learn the notes, the intervals, the way a rhythm holds, and only then can you stop counting and simply play. What sounds like instinct is knowledge that has settled.

You need no experience. Come and play with us, or come and listen and do nothing at all. Either way, come as you are.

Before you
come.

What is a sound journey?
Our name for a sound bath. You lie on a mat, close your eyes, and we play handpan, gong, and Tibetan and crystal bowls live around you for 75 minutes. Nothing to learn, nothing to say.
Do I need any experience or background?
No. Nothing is asked of you beyond lying down. Never been near a gong or a handpan before is the usual starting point here.
Where is it, and how do I find it?
A covered open-air terrace in Lamai, five minutes inland from the beach. Park at the bottom and take the stairs up, following the music. We send a short video for finding it once you book.
How much does it cost?
Lessons and private journeys are priced in Learn Handpan and Sound Journeys. Everything with a date is priced in What’s On.
What should I wear and bring?
Loose clothes you can lie down in, and a light layer, because an hour of stillness leaves you cooler than you expect. The mats are here.
What happens if it rains?
Sessions go ahead. The terrace has a roof, so the only difference is the rain on it, which is no bad thing to lie under.
How do I book, and how far ahead?
Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram and we usually reply within a few hours. A day or two ahead is normally enough, though there are only eight mats. Give us a week for high season, the full moon session and private evenings.
Can I leave in the middle?
Yes, whenever you like. Stand up and go, with no explanation needed. Sitting up on your mat is fine too, if lying down stops being comfortable.
