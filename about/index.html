Who we are

Can and Melie — the two people who play.

One of us plays handpan and teaches it. One of us plays gong and bowls and teaches yoga. We hold every session together on a terrace in Lamai.

Can

Handpan, rhythm, and theory.

Can is Turkish. He started playing handpan in 2016 and began teaching in 2024. He plays handpan, bowls and drums.

He is certified in Integral Sound Healing by the Academy of Sound Healing (2024) and in Advanced Sound Facilitation by Shantika Academy (2025). He holds certificates for both.

He has held over one hundred sessions, in Turkey and here on Samui. He teaches three or four new students most months, and works from how sound behaves: which notes sit well together, why a bowl keeps ringing after you let it go, how a room changes what you hear.

He moved to Samui in June 2025. He had a commercial career in Turkey and left it to spend his time on handpan teaching and sound therapy instead.

Melie

Gong, bowls, and yoga.

Melie studied public health and worked in hospitality before she trained as a yoga teacher in Koh Samui. She holds a 200-hour Vikasa Yoga certification. That training is where she heard sound healing for the first time.

She then completed a 100-hour sound therapy course with Shantika. She has taught yoga at Shantika Academy to more than fifty students.

She plays gong, crystal bowls and Tibetan bowls. She also runs House of Holistic by Melie, which offers sound therapy, yoga, guided meditation and Tok Sen massage.

Together

Two players, one session.

We have held sound journeys together for over a year, more than fifty sessions so far. Playing as a pair is the reason the sound never drops to silence: one of us can hold a long tone while the other moves around the mats, so the instruments answer each other instead of arriving one at a time.

Can plays handpan, bowls and drums. Melie plays gong, crystal bowls and Tibetan bowls. In a session that means the melody and the long tones are held by different pairs of hands, and we can hand the room back and forth without stopping.

We also teach. Can takes three or four new handpan students most months, and teaches rhythm and music theory with the instrument. Melie teaches yoga, and taught yoga at Shantika Academy to more than fifty students. Between us we hold the open sessions on the terrace, private sessions for couples and small groups, and sessions at villas, hotels and retreats across the island.

We have been invited to play at Kamalaya, Anantara Lawana, W, Conrad, Absolute Sanctuary, Samujana, Vikasa, 5 Elements and Samaya Wellness, along with yoga studios and community spaces across the island. We hold a place in the Kamalaya wellness programme.

How it started

Noom Sound Studio, and the terrace.

Noom Sound Studio began with a handpan, a gong, and a terrace nobody was using. Can was teaching lessons on it. Melie brought bowls to one of them. We played together, saw that it worked, and started running open sessions on Wednesday and Sunday.

The terrace sits five minutes inland from Lamai beach, in the forest above the sea. It is open on three sides and covered overhead, so sessions run in the rain. Eight mats fit on it, and we have never wanted more. Park at the bottom, take the stairs, and follow the music.

Everything we do runs from there: sound journeys, handpan lessons, and sessions at villas and hotels across Koh Samui. Sessions run in English.

You need no experience for any of it. Come and play with us, or come and listen and do nothing at all. Either way, come as you are.
